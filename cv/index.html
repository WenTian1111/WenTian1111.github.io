Research experience

ESI-Curvature Modeling of Egg Trajectories: Morphological Effects (Independent Research)

Jun 2025 – Jun 2026

Built a standardized image acquisition platform with a 2.87-degree inclined flexible-contact surface; collected static contours of 90 eggs and 270 rolling videos. Extracted 11 static morphological features (ESI, asymmetry index, Hu moments) and 3 dynamic indicators via machine vision, with vision-based ESI reaching 0.788% mean error vs. manual measurement. Fused dynamic indicators into a fragility index (RSI 0-100) via PCA and K-means, trained SVM/RF/GBDT/LR classifiers with SVM selected as optimal, and deployed the web application Egg_RSI_App (Streamlit Cloud) for rolling-risk prediction.

  • Provincial-level Innovation & Entrepreneurship Training Program — Excellent completion

Machine Vision for Coupled Egg Dynamics & Intelligent Sorting (Leader)

May 2025 – May 2026

Led and supervised a four-member team in machine-vision-based egg analysis and sorting theses: developed kinematic time-series extraction (centroid, attitude, velocity, acceleration), coupled static morphology with dynamic rolling for risk prediction via a PCA-based rolling stability index, trained four ML classifiers with SVM optimal (74.07% on 90 eggs, 270 videos), clustered trajectories into three stability classes via GMM, and built multi-species egg classifiers from fused HSV and LBP features (8 species, 713 images; KNN 99.30%).

  • Graduation thesis project of a four-member group, completed successfully

Research on Multi-Object Localization of Rocket Debris (Independent Research)

May 2024 – Aug 2024

Developed a rocket debris localization and landing-point prediction strategy based on sonic-boom signals: WGS84-to-ECEF conversion, an improved tri-sphere and quad-sphere intersection model solved by nonlinear least squares (MATLAB lsqnonlin), a traversal algorithm over 256 time combinations with feasibility filtering, and weighted least squares with Monte Carlo simulation to analyze localization stability under 0.5 s timing errors.

  • Published in Frontier and Progress of Computational Mathematics and Modeling (2024, vol. 7)

Various Mathematical Modeling Competitions (Leader; Independent Research)

Mar 2023 – Jan 2026

Led teams or worked independently on competition papers: the 2024 Certified Cup Challenge (finite-element skull model based on the Ogden constitutive model), the 2024 CUMCM (helical coiling dragon simulation via polar-angle iteration and Simpson's rule), and the 2025 CUMCM (multi-objective UAV smoke-screen jamming simulation optimized by particle swarm with dual-plane projection occlusion modeling).

  • First-class awards at both national and provincial levels
