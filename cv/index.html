Research experience

ESI-Curvature Modeling of Egg Trajectories: Morphological Effects (Independent Research)

Jun 2025 – Jun 2026

Built a controlled 2.87-degree rolling platform and collected static images of 90 eggs with three trials each (270 videos). Extracted morphology and motion descriptors; vision-based ESI showed 0.788% mean relative error against manual measurement. Constructed a motion-based RSI using PCA and K-means and compared four classifiers. The final thesis reports SVM accuracy of 74.07% and macro F1 of 81.64%, while gradient boosting achieved the highest macro AUC (86.65%). Results use a trial-level split; independent-egg validation remains a next step. Developed the companion Egg_RSI_App research demonstration.

  • Provincial-level Innovation & Entrepreneurship Training Program — Excellent completion

Machine Vision for Coupled Egg Dynamics & Intelligent Sorting (Leader)

May 2025 – May 2026

Led and supervised a four-member team in machine-vision-based egg analysis and sorting theses: developed kinematic time-series extraction (centroid, attitude, velocity, acceleration), coupled static morphology with dynamic rolling for risk prediction via a PCA-based rolling stability index, trained four ML classifiers with SVM leading on reported accuracy (74.07%, trial-level split of 270 videos from 90 eggs), clustered trajectories into three stability classes via GMM, and built multi-species egg classifiers from fused HSV and LBP features (8 species, 713 images; KNN 99.30%).

  • Graduation thesis project of a four-member group, completed successfully

Research on Multi-Object Localization of Rocket Debris (Independent Research)

May 2024 – Aug 2024

Developed a numerical sonic-boom source localization workflow using approximate WGS84-based coordinate preparation, nonlinear least squares (MATLAB lsqnonlin), and a search over 256 arrival-time combinations with feasibility filtering. Studied four-source association and sensitivity to recording noise with a 0.5 s standard deviation; the results estimate airborne boom positions, not validated landing points.

  • Published in Frontier and Progress of Computational Mathematics and Modeling (December 2024, no. 6)

Mathematical Modeling Competitions & Research Series (Independent Research)

Independently formulated and solved an eight-part applied mathematical modeling series spanning marine bathymetric surveying, renewable microgrid storage coordination, linked-body spiral kinematics (bench-dragon dynamics), CNC cutting-path optimization, multi-channel sensor navigation, public-health care networks, fiber-optic curve reconstruction, and constrained geometric simulation. Formulated mathematical frameworks coupling nonlinear optimization, computational geometry, statistical estimation, and Monte Carlo sensitivity analysis; responsible for end-to-end mathematical modeling, algorithm implementation, numerical simulation, and manuscript preparation.
