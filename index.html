I am an incoming M.A. student in Religious Studies at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), and a recent graduate of Southwest University where I majored in Animal Science and Applied Mathematics. My academic vision is to bridge ancient Chinese philosophical frameworks with modern scientific inquiry, exploring the fundamental logic underlying the universe.

During my undergraduate years, I developed strong analytical skills through mathematical modeling competitions and hands-on research — from trajectory analysis and optical sensing to semiconductor thermal modeling. I also completed a provincial-level College Students’ Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program as a solo researcher, applying machine vision and data-driven methods to agricultural engineering.

Beyond the lab, I have a deep interest in the Yijing, Daoism, and traditional Chinese cosmology. My long-term research goal is to bridge ancient Chinese philosophical frameworks with modern scientific inquiry, exploring the fundamental logic underlying the universe. Research Experience

  • ESI-Curvature Modeling of Egg Trajectories: Morphological Effects (Leader)
    • Apr. 2025 - Apr. 2026
    • Led a team to design an egg rolling test rig with high-speed cameras and MATLAB-based data analysis. Innovatively converted 3D motion into 2D planar motion, utilizing Kalman filtering to extract dynamic behavioral features. Applied polynomial fitting to analyze the impact of egg shape index and eccentricity on trajectory, providing a basis for egg sorting.
    • Output: Project approved at university level, pushing for National Undergraduate Innovation Program. One JCR Q1 paper planned.
  • Machine Vision for Coupled Egg Dynamics & Intelligent Sorting (Leader)
    • May 2025 - May 2026
    • Led literature review on coupling mechanism of egg morphology, contact interface characteristics, and dynamic rolling behavior. Systematically summarized 240 recent review articles and drafted initial review manuscript.
    • Output: One JCR Q1 review paper planned.
  • Various Mathematical Modeling Competitions (Leader)
    • May 2023 - May 2025
    • Led team in paper writing, modeling, and programming. Notable: 2024 Certified Cup - Finite Element Model of Skull based on Ogden Constitutive Model; 2024 National CUMCM - Simulation Design of Helical Coiling Dragon Activity using polar angle iteration and Simpson’s numerical integration.
    • Output: First-class awards at national and provincial levels.
  • Research on Multi-Object Localization of Rocket Debris (Independent Research)
    • May 2024 - Aug. 2024
    • Developed rocket debris recovery strategy using sonic booms by optimizing WGS84-based tri-sphere and quad-sphere intersection models. Employed MATLAB with lsqnonlin and novel traversal algorithm for weighted least squares correction, validated by Monte Carlo simulations.
    • Output: Published paper in Frontier and Progress of Computational Mathematics and Modeling, 2024, vol. 7, 01-24.
