Designed and implemented an egg rolling test rig with high-speed cameras and MATLAB-based data analysis to model egg trajectory dynamics. Developed curvature-to-trajectory models integrating spline interpolation and numerical integration. Completed as part of the National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program (solo project, provincial-level).
