### Research Experience

*   **ESI-Curvature Modeling of Egg Trajectories** (Leader)
    *   Designed and implemented an egg rolling test rig with high-speed cameras and MATLAB-based data analysis to model egg trajectory dynamics. Developed curvature-to-trajectory models integrating spline interpolation and numerical integration. Completed as part of the National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program (solo project, provincial-level).

*   **Fiber Optic Sensor-Based Plane Curve Reconstruction** (Leader)
    *   Reconstructed 2D curved beam shapes from fiber Bragg grating (FBG) strain data. Developed wavelength-to-curvature transformation models and performed arc-length parameterized integration to recover spatial coordinates. Addressed sensor spacing optimization and error propagation across multi-segment reconstructions.

*   **Chip Thermal-Elastic Parameter Estimation for PCBA** (Leader)
    *   Built finite element models to estimate effective elastic modulus and thermal expansion coefficients of BGA solder joints under thermal cycling. Combined analytical beam theory with numerical optimization to predict corner stress concentration and fatigue failure in electronic packaging.

*   **Multi-Object Localization of Rocket Debris** (Independent Research)
    *   Developed a rocket debris recovery strategy using sonic booms by optimizing WGS84-based tri-sphere and quad-sphere intersection models. Employed MATLAB, including lsqnonlin and a novel traversal algorithm, to implement a weighted least squares correction model. Validated by Monte Carlo simulations, confirming the necessity of at least four monitoring devices.
    *   Output: Paper titled "Research on Multi-Object Localization of Rocket Debris."
